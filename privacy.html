Privacy Policy

Financial Planner · Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Financial Planner ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. We built this app to help you plan a budget, track expenses, set savings goals, and organize your money. We take a privacy-first approach: we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Account information. If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google, we receive your email address (and, from Google sign-in, your basic profile identifier). This is used only to save and sync your data across your devices.
  • Financial information you enter. The income, expenses, budget categories, savings goals, deposits, and any investment notes that you type into the app. We do not connect to your bank; nothing is imported automatically. This information is only what you choose to enter.
  • App preferences. Basic settings such as language and your chosen tab.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers.

Your app lock (PIN and biometrics)

If you set up a PIN or Face ID / fingerprint unlock, that protection is stored only on your device. Your PIN is stored in a hashed form and is never sent to our servers. Biometric verification is handled entirely by your device's operating system; we never receive your face or fingerprint data.

How your information is stored

  • On your device. Your data is saved locally in your browser/app storage so the app works and remembers your information.
  • Cloud backup (optional). If you are signed in, a copy of your data is stored so you can restore it on a new device. This backup is hosted using Supabase, a cloud database provider acting as our data processor. Sign-in is handled through Supabase authentication and Google.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features (budgeting, goals, tracking).
  • To save your data and sync it across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To keep the app secure (for example, the optional PIN lock).

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the service providers required to run the app:

  • Supabase — cloud database and authentication (stores your backup so you can restore it).
  • Google — only if you choose "Sign in with Google," to verify your identity.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices and controls

  • Use without an account. You can use the app locally without signing in; in that case your data stays on your device only.
  • Export. You can export a backup of your data from the app's Settings.
  • Delete. You can reset the app from Settings to erase your data on the device. To request deletion of your cloud backup and account, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

Data retention

Your data is kept as long as you use the app or keep an account. If you reset the app, local data is removed. If you request account deletion, we remove your cloud backup.

Security

Data sent to the cloud is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children's privacy

Financial Planner is intended for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a minor, please use the app with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

International users

Your information may be processed on servers operated by our providers in other countries. By using the app, you consent to this processing. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data — contact us to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
